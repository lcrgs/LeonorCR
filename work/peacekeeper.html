Brief

For the Advanced Topics in Human-Computer Interaction module of my MSc at Glasgow Caledonian University, I designed The Peacekeeper: an AI mediator that de-escalates polarised conversations. By detecting emotions and triggering interventions, it guides two people from defensiveness toward empathy.

Roles

- Interaction Designer & Researcher (Solo Project)
- Created personas, a scenario and storyboards.
- Designed the device, the conversation sequence and the affective feedback loop.
- Planned a lab study to evaluate the system.

The Problem

Two people cannot communicate because fear and anger have replaced curiosity and empathy. David, a traditional father, and Julie, his transgender daughter, haven't truly talked in three years, and Christmas dinner ends in shouting.

- Lack of emotional safety: Each side feels attacked, so nobody listens.
- Lack of structure: Without guidance, the conversation spirals into blame.

The Solution

- The device: A neutral cylinder between the two people, with cameras and microphones that capture voice, facial expressions, posture and turn-taking.
- Emotion detection: Arousal and valence are mapped onto Russell's Circumplex Model to recognise six emotions: fear, anger, frustration, contempt, disgust and shame.
- Structured sequence: Onboarding, Provocation, Storytelling, Reverse (speaking for the other person) and Reflect.
- Interventions: Each emotion triggers a matching response, such as pausing, redirecting or a reset with silence, in a continuous Sense, Analyse, Map, Intervene feedback loop.

Evaluation Plan

- Research question: Does The Peacekeeper de-escalate polarised conversations and guide participants toward empathy?
- Study: A lab study with 30 adults in 15 pairs holding opposing views, with 40-50 minute sessions.
- Measures: Detection accuracy against expert analysis (F1-score), arousal before and after each intervention, and self-reported empathy through SAM and PANAS questionnaires.
- Ethics: Informed consent, anonymised data, the right to withdraw and mental health resources at debrief.
